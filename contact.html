Response Time

We typically respond within 2–3 business days. For urgent bug reports, please include "URGENT" in your subject line.

Bug Reports

If you've found a bug, please include the following information to help us diagnose and fix the issue:

  • Device and browser: e.g., iPhone 15 Pro / Safari 17, or Windows 11 / Chrome 120
  • Steps to reproduce: What you were doing when the bug occurred
  • Expected behavior: What you thought should happen
  • Actual behavior: What actually happened
  • Screenshots: If applicable, include a screenshot of the issue
  • Console errors: Open your browser's developer tools (F12) and check the Console tab for any error messages
